Strip alignment — photos + PNG

ONE SCAN PER STRIP

Print a numbered reference sheet, then scan its four cut strips separately. The reference marks measure where the whole design lands relative to each paper edge. One correction moves and scales photos and PNG together. Separate photo centering is no longer used for new strips.

  1. Prepare and print the reference sheet below. Keep the four numbered strips.
  2. Scan each strip at 300 or 600 DPI with a dark matte sheet behind it. Leave visible backing around all four paper edges. Turn off automatic cropping, resizing and perspective correction.
  3. Upload strips 1–4, check the green outlines trace the actual cut paper, then calculate and review the correction.

Negative scanned margins mean an edge would be outside the paper. Margins are approximate millimeters at 300 DPI. Green outlines must follow the paper edge, not a printed border. Scanner detection and printer repeatability limit accuracy.

Advanced: manual strip alignment

Usually leave these scan-derived values alone. Manual edits replace the same alignment; they never add another correction. Each strip moves and scales as one piece, including its photos and PNG.
